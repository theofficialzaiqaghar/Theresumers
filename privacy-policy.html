Privacy policy

How we handle information when you use TheResumer.co.uk and contact us.

Who we are

TheResumer (“we”, “us”) provides professional CV writing, LinkedIn profile management, and career content services. This policy explains what personal data we collect and how we use it when you visit theresumer.co.uk or get in touch.

Information we collect

We may collect:

  • Contact details — name, email, phone number, and any information you submit via our contact form or email.
  • Career information — CVs, LinkedIn URLs, job targets, and project notes you voluntarily share for a writing engagement.
  • Technical data — basic browser/device information if analytics or hosting logs are enabled on the live site (this static preview may not collect analytics until configured).

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide requested services
  • Prepare, revise, and deliver CV, LinkedIn, and content work
  • Maintain records for ongoing client relationships where applicable
  • Improve our website and service quality

We do not sell your personal data.

Legal basis (UK)

Where UK GDPR applies, we process personal data on the basis of contract performance, legitimate interests in operating our business, and consent where you have given it (for example, when submitting a form).

Retention

Enquiry records are kept only as long as needed to respond and administer projects. Client work files may be retained for a reasonable period to support revisions or repeat engagements unless you request deletion.

Sharing

We may share data with trusted providers who help us operate email, hosting, or form processing—only to the extent needed and under appropriate safeguards. We do not share career documents for marketing without your permission.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorised access. No online transmission is completely secure; please avoid sending highly sensitive data unless necessary for your project.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data, or object to certain processing, subject to applicable law. Contact us at support@theresumer.co.uk.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@theresumer.co.uk or contact form.
