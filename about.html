Our story

Built for the unique GCC job market

Employers in Saudi Arabia and the Gulf look for specific qualifications, keywords, and presentation—a generic, one-size-fits-all CV will not make the cut. THE RESUMER closes that gap with documents recruiters actually want to read.

We are ATS and GCC employer optimised, culturally aware in how we tell your story, and industry-specific across Engineering, Healthcare, Finance, IT, Construction, and more—with Arabic and English options and 20+ active recruiter partnerships across the region.

Mission

Clarity first. Confidence always.

Our mission is simple: help you present your experience honestly and powerfully, without hype or filler. We listen, ask the right questions, and translate your background into language that fits your industry and ambition.

Every project is handled with confidentiality. You stay in control of your narrative—we provide structure, polish, and perspective.

  • 20+ recruiter partnerships across Saudi Arabia
  • Bilingual Arabic and English professional writing
  • Fast turnaround with confidentiality guaranteed
